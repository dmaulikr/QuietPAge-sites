Some questions return.

Twelve of QuietPage's questions are Mirror questions — prompts that come back across weeks and months. When you finish writing one, the app shows you what you said the last few times you answered it. Side by side. Silent.

It is the small, private moment that no other journal gives you: the chance to read your past self in the act of becoming someone slightly different.

Quiet by design.

No streaks

A page from before

Read your earlier self.

Now and then, beneath today's question, QuietPage surfaces a single sentence from a page you wrote weeks or months ago — quietly, without ceremony. A reason to return that isn't a reminder.
